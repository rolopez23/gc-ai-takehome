MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

Vendor: Pulsar Platforms, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 3000 Innovation Way, Suite 700, Atlanta, GA 30303 ("Vendor"), and

Customer: Northgate Consulting Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 101 Federal Street, Suite 1400, Boston, MA 02110 ("Customer").

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents who are authorized to access the Services under this Agreement.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, content, and information submitted to or collected by the Services on behalf of Customer.

1.3 "Documentation" means the technical documentation and user guides provided by Vendor describing the Services.

1.4 "Output" means any content, report, analysis, or other result generated by the Services using Customer Data as an input.

1.5 "Order Form" means a written ordering document executed by both parties that incorporates this Agreement and specifies the Services, fees, and Subscription Term.

1.6 "Personal Data" has the meaning given under applicable data protection laws.

1.7 "Services" means the software-as-a-service applications and related services described in the applicable Order Form.

1.8 "Subscription Term" means the duration of Customer's access to the Services as set forth in an Order Form.

---

2. SERVICES

2.1 Provision of Services. Vendor will make the Services available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, and the Documentation.

2.2 Availability. Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the availability of the Services. Vendor does not guarantee any specific uptime level or service response time and makes no binding service level commitments. Scheduled maintenance will be communicated at least 24 hours in advance by email.

2.3 Support. Vendor will provide standard technical support via email and ticket portal during business hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM Eastern Time, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).

2.4 Modifications. Vendor may modify, update, or discontinue the Services, including pricing, features, or usage policies, at any time upon 10 days' prior written notice to Customer. Customer's continued use of the Services following the notice period will constitute Customer's acceptance of such modifications. Vendor reserves the right to apply such changes mid-Subscription Term.

---

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees. Customer will pay Vendor the fees specified in the applicable Order Form. All fees are in U.S. dollars.

3.2 Invoicing and Payment. Vendor will invoice Customer per the billing schedule in the Order Form. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3.3 Taxes. Customer is responsible for all applicable taxes, excluding taxes on Vendor's net income.

3.4 Fee Adjustments. Vendor may adjust fees at the beginning of any renewal Subscription Term on 60 days' written notice. In addition, pursuant to Section 2.4, Vendor may adjust pricing mid-term upon 10 days' written notice, and Customer's continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance of such adjusted pricing.

---

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

4.1 Vendor IP. Vendor retains all rights, title, and interest in and to the Services, Documentation, underlying technology, and all improvements and derivative works thereof.

4.2 Customer Data. Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Data. Vendor acquires no ownership interest in Customer Data as such.

4.3 Output Ownership. Any Output generated by the Services using Customer Data as input is jointly owned by Vendor and Customer. Each party may freely use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and commercialize any Output without accounting to the other, subject to applicable law. Vendor may use Output in its marketing and promotional materials, in aggregate analytics and industry reports, and for product development and improvement purposes, including but not limited to training and fine-tuning AI and machine learning models.

4.4 License to Customer Data. Customer grants Vendor a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to access and use Customer Data to provide the Services and as otherwise set forth in this Agreement, including the Output ownership and commercialization rights in Section 4.3.

4.5 Restrictions. Vendor will not sell raw, un-processed Customer Data as a standalone data product to third parties without Customer's prior written consent.

4.6 Feedback. Customer grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use any feedback provided by Customer regarding the Services.

---

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood as confidential.

5.2 Obligations. The Receiving Party will: (a) protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care; (b) use Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; and (c) limit disclosure to employees and contractors with a need to know, under comparable obligations.

5.3 Exceptions. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known without restriction; (c) is independently developed; or (d) is required by law to be disclosed.

5.4 Duration. Confidentiality obligations will terminate upon expiration or termination of this Agreement. Neither party will have ongoing confidentiality obligations after the Agreement ends.

---

6. DATA PROCESSING AND PRIVACY

6.1 Data Processing Agreement. Vendor's data processing practices are governed by the Data Processing Agreement located at https://www.pulsarplatforms.com/legal/dpa, which may be updated by Vendor from time to time. Customer's continued use of the Services following any such update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

6.2 Data Deletion. Upon termination, Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to delete Customer Data from its live systems within a commercially reasonable timeframe. No specific deletion timeline is guaranteed. Vendor may retain anonymized and aggregated data derived from Customer Data indefinitely.

6.3 Compliance. Each party will comply with applicable data protection laws.

---

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages. IN NO EVENT WILL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, GOODWILL, OR DATA.

7.2 Aggregate Cap. EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

7.3 Acknowledgment. The parties agree that the limitations in this Section 7 represent a reasonable allocation of risk and form an essential element of the basis of the bargain.

---

8. INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Customer Indemnification. Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor and its officers, directors, and employees from any third-party claims arising out of or relating to: (a) Customer Data; (b) Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law; or (c) Customer's breach of any representation or warranty under this Agreement.

8.2 No Vendor Indemnification. Vendor does not provide any indemnification to Customer against third-party intellectual property claims or otherwise. Customer accepts the Services on an "as-is" basis with respect to third-party IP exposure.

8.3 Procedure. The indemnified party must promptly notify the indemnifying party of any claim and provide reasonable cooperation.

---

9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 Agreement Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

9.2 Subscription Renewal. Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term, each Order Form will automatically renew for successive one-year terms at then-current rates.

9.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice of a material breach if the breach is not cured within that period.

9.4 Termination for Convenience. Vendor may terminate this Agreement for any reason on 60 days' notice. Customer may not terminate for convenience during a Subscription Term.

9.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access to the Services will cease. Customer Data deletion will be handled in accordance with Section 6.2. Vendor is under no obligation to provide Customer with an export of Customer Data following termination.

9.6 Survival. Sections 1, 4.1, 4.3, 4.4, 5.4, 7, 8, and 10 will survive termination.

10.1 Governing Law and Venue. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to conflict of law principles. Disputes will be resolved in the state or federal courts of Fulton County, Georgia.

Customer's continued use of the Services after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the amended terms. Vendor reserves the right to amend pricing, features, usage policies, and other material terms mid-term pursuant to this provision.

10.4 Waiver. No failure to enforce any right will constitute a waiver of that right.

10.5 Severability. If any provision is found unenforceable, it will be modified to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will remain in effect.

10.8 Force Majeure. Neither party will be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
