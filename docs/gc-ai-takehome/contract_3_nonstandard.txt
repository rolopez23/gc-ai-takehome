MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

Vendor: Stratavox Systems, LLC, a Texas limited liability company, 200 River Walk Plaza, Suite 900, San Antonio, TX 78205 ("Vendor"), and

Customer: Crestfield Media Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 450 Fifth Avenue, 12th Floor, New York, NY 10018 ("Customer").

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Aggregated Usage Data" means Customer Data that has been de-identified, aggregated, and anonymized such that it cannot reasonably be used to identify Customer or any individual, and from which all proprietary or competitively sensitive content has been removed.
1.2 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents permitted to access the Services under this Agreement.
1.3 "Customer Data" means all data, content, and information submitted to or collected by the Services on Customer's behalf, including Personal Data.
1.4 "Documentation" means technical specifications, user guides, and written materials provided by Vendor describing the Services.
1.5 "Order Form" means a written ordering document executed by both parties specifying the Services, Fees, and Subscription Term.
1.6 "Output" means any content, report, analysis, or result generated by the Services using Customer Data as input.
1.7 "Personal Data" has the meaning given under applicable data protection laws.
1.8 "Services" means the software-as-a-service products and related services described in an applicable Order Form.
1.9 "Subscription Term" means the period during which Customer has purchased access to the Services, as specified in the Order Form.

---

2. SERVICES

2.1 Provision. Vendor will make the Services available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, and the Documentation.

2.2 Uptime SLA. Vendor guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime availability, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 72 hours in advance. If Vendor fails to meet this target in any calendar month, Customer will receive a service credit equal to 10% of that month's pro-rated Fees for each full percentage point below 99.9%, up to 30% of monthly Fees. Service credits are Customer's exclusive remedy for uptime failures.

2.3 Support SLA. Vendor will provide technical support with the following response time commitments: (a) Critical (service unavailable or data at risk): initial response within 4 hours, 24/7; (b) High (material feature impairment): response within 1 business day; (c) Standard: response within 3 business days. Business hours are 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

2.4 Data Security Certifications. Vendor will maintain SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications throughout the Subscription Term and will provide current reports or executive summaries to Customer upon written request. Vendor will notify Customer within 10 business days if any certification lapses.

2.5 Subprocessors. Vendor maintains a current list of subprocessors used to provide the Services, which will be disclosed to Customer at execution. Vendor requires Customer's prior written consent before engaging any new subprocessor with access to Customer Data. Vendor will provide at least 30 days' advance written notice of any proposed new subprocessor, and Customer may object in writing within 15 days.

2.6 Change Management. Vendor will provide at least 30 days' prior written notice of any material change to the features or functionality of the Services that would adversely affect Customer's use.

---

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees. Customer will pay the fees specified in the applicable Order Form ("Fees"). All Fees are in U.S. dollars and are non-refundable except as expressly provided.

3.2 Payment Terms. Vendor will invoice Customer per the billing schedule in the Order Form. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt (Net 45). Customer may dispute any invoice in good faith within 15 days without forfeiting obligations on undisputed amounts. Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month.

3.3 Price Increase Cap. Vendor may increase Fees at the start of any renewal Subscription Term upon at least 60 days' prior written notice. Annual increases are capped at the lesser of 3% or the change in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for the preceding 12 months. No Fee increases may be applied during a then-current Subscription Term without Customer's prior written consent.

3.4 Taxes. Customer is responsible for all applicable sales, use, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on Vendor's net income.

---

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

4.1 Vendor IP. Vendor retains all rights, title, and interest in and to the Services, Documentation, underlying technology, and improvements thereto.

4.2 Customer Data and Output. Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Data and all Output. Vendor acquires no ownership interest in Customer Data or Output.

4.3 License to Provide Services. Customer grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and process Customer Data solely to provide the Services during the Subscription Term.

4.4 License to Aggregated Usage Data. Customer grants Vendor a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use Aggregated Usage Data for the following limited purposes only: (a) improving the performance, reliability, and security of the Services; (b) developing new features and functionality; and (c) generating aggregate, non-customer-identifiable industry benchmarking reports shared with third parties. This license expressly excludes: (i) identifying or re-identifying Customer or any individual from Aggregated Usage Data; (ii) using Aggregated Usage Data for targeted advertising directed at Customer or its users; (iii) selling Aggregated Usage Data as a standalone data product; and (iv) any use that would violate applicable privacy law. Vendor will maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent re-identification.

4.5 Restrictions on Customer Data. Vendor will not use Customer Data to train or fine-tune AI or machine learning models in a manner that would enable a model to reproduce Customer's proprietary content or strategies. Vendor will not sell raw Customer Data to any third party.

4.6 Feedback. If Customer provides feedback regarding the Services, Vendor may use such feedback without restriction, provided it does not incorporate Customer's Confidential Information or identify Customer without consent.

---

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Obligations. Each party will hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care as for its own confidential information (no less than reasonable care), and will use it only to perform obligations or exercise rights under this Agreement. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information designated as confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential given the circumstances.

5.2 Exceptions. Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known without restriction; (c) is independently developed; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, with prompt prior notice where permitted.

5.3 Survival. Confidentiality obligations survive expiration or termination of this Agreement for three (3) years.

---

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 Vendor Warranties. Vendor warrants that: (a) the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the Subscription Term; (b) Vendor will provide the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner; (c) Vendor will comply with all applicable laws in providing the Services; and (d) to Vendor's knowledge, the Services do not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

6.2 Mutual Warranties. Each party warrants that it has full legal authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.3 Warranty Remedy. If the Services fail to conform to the warranties in Section 6.1(a), Customer will notify Vendor and Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity at no charge within 30 days. If uncorrected, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid, unused Fees.

6.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 6, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

7.1 Data Processing Agreement. The terms governing Vendor's processing of Personal Data on Customer's behalf — including security measures, subprocessor obligations, data subject rights, and cross-border transfer mechanisms — are set forth in the Data Processing Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A, incorporated by reference. Any amendment to Exhibit A requires the written consent of both parties.

7.2 Compliance. Each party will comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws in connection with its activities under this Agreement.

8. AUDIT RIGHTS

8.1 Customer may, no more than once per calendar year and upon at least 30 days' prior written notice, audit or commission an independent third-party audit of Vendor's systems and records to verify compliance with this Agreement, at Customer's cost. Vendor will cooperate reasonably and provide access to relevant documentation. Customer will treat all findings as Vendor's Confidential Information.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 Vendor will maintain throughout the Subscription Term: (a) commercial general liability of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) professional liability / errors and omissions of at least $2,000,000; and (c) cyber liability / technology errors and omissions of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence. Upon request, Vendor will provide certificates of insurance.

---

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Consequential Damages. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY, IP, OR DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS OR DATA.

10.2 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 10.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

10.3 Excluded Claims. The cap does not apply to: (a) indemnification obligations; (b) confidentiality breaches; (c) data protection violations; or (d) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

---

11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Vendor Indemnification. Vendor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from any third-party claim alleging that the Services, used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, or arising from Vendor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11.2 Customer Indemnification. Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor from any third-party claim arising out of Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law.

11.3 Procedure. The indemnified party must promptly notify the indemnifying party, grant sole control of defense and settlement (with consent for any settlement imposing obligations on the indemnified party), and provide reasonable cooperation.

---

12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

12.2 Auto-Renewal. Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term, each Order Form will automatically renew for successive one-year terms at then-current rates, subject to Section 3.3.

12.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice of a material breach if not cured within the notice period.

12.4 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for any reason on 30 days' written notice.

12.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access ceases. Vendor will provide a complete, machine-readable export of Customer Data within 30 days and will securely delete Customer Data within 60 days, unless retention is required by applicable law. Vendor will provide written certification of deletion upon request.

12.6 Exit Assistance. Upon Customer's written request, Vendor will provide reasonable transition assistance for up to 90 days following termination, at no additional charge, to facilitate orderly migration of Customer Data and Services.

12.7 Survival. Sections 1, 4.1, 4.2, 5, 6.4, 7, 10, 11, and 13 survive termination.

13. GENERAL

13.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in San Antonio, Texas.

13.2 Dispute Resolution. Before initiating formal proceedings, the parties will: (a) attempt good-faith senior-level negotiations for 30 days; then (b) submit to non-binding JAMS mediation for 30 days; then (c) resolve by binding JAMS arbitration under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, single arbitrator, San Antonio, Texas. Either party may seek emergency injunctive relief without first completing these steps.

13.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, all Order Forms, and Exhibits constitute the entire agreement and supersede all prior understandings.

13.4 Amendment. No amendment is valid unless signed in writing by both parties.

13.5 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

13.6 Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, excluding financial hardship and reasonably foreseeable events. The affected party must give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

13.7 Notices. Notices must be in writing, delivered by email with confirmation, overnight courier, or certified mail to addresses in the Order Form.
