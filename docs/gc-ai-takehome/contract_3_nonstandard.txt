MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

Vendor: Stratavox Systems, LLC, a Texas limited liability company, 200 River Walk Plaza, Suite 900, San Antonio, TX 78205 ("Vendor"), and

Customer: Crestfield Media Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 450 Fifth Avenue, 12th Floor, New York, NY 10018 ("Customer").

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents permitted to access the Services under this Agreement.
1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, content, and information submitted to or collected by the Services on Customer's behalf, including Personal Data.
1.3 "Documentation" means technical specifications, user guides, and written materials provided by Vendor describing the Services.
1.4 "Order Form" means a written ordering document executed by both parties specifying the Services, Fees, and Subscription Term.
1.5 "Output" means any content, report, analysis, or result generated by the Services using Customer Data as input.
1.6 "Personal Data" has the meaning given under applicable data protection laws.
1.7 "Services" means the software-as-a-service products and related services described in an applicable Order Form.
1.8 "Subscription Term" means the period during which Customer has purchased access to the Services, as specified in the Order Form.

---

2. SERVICES

2.1 Provision. Vendor will make the Services available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, and the Documentation.

2.2 Uptime SLA. Vendor targets 99.5% monthly uptime availability, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 48 hours in advance. If Vendor fails to meet this target in any calendar month, Customer will receive a service credit equal to 5% of that month's pro-rated Fees for each full percentage point below 99.5%, up to 15% of monthly Fees. Service credits are Customer's exclusive remedy for uptime failures.

2.3 Support SLA. Vendor will provide technical support during business hours (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays). Critical issues: initial response within 8 business hours. High: response within 2 business days. Standard: response within 5 business days. No 24/7 support is included.

2.4 Data Security. Vendor will maintain commercially reasonable security measures consistent with industry standards. Vendor will notify Customer within 30 business days if any material security certification lapses.

2.5 Subprocessors. Vendor maintains a list of subprocessors used to provide the Services. Vendor will notify Customer within 15 days of engaging any new subprocessor. Customer may object in writing within 10 days, but Vendor retains final discretion over subprocessor selection.

2.6 Change Management. Vendor will provide at least 15 days' prior written notice of any material change to the features or functionality of the Services.

---

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees. Customer will pay the fees specified in the applicable Order Form ("Fees"). All Fees are in U.S. dollars and are non-refundable except as expressly provided.

3.2 Payment Terms. Vendor will invoice Customer per the billing schedule in the Order Form. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt (Net 30). Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3.3 Price Adjustments. Vendor may increase Fees at the start of any renewal Subscription Term upon at least 45 days' prior written notice. Annual increases are capped at 7%.

3.4 Taxes. Customer is responsible for all applicable sales, use, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on Vendor's net income.

---

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

4.1 Vendor IP. Vendor retains all rights, title, and interest in and to the Services, Documentation, underlying technology, and improvements thereto.

4.2 Customer Data and Output. Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Data. Output generated by the Services is jointly owned by Customer and Vendor; however, Vendor will not use Output in any way that identifies Customer without prior written consent.

4.3 License to Provide Services. Customer grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and process Customer Data solely to provide the Services during the Subscription Term.

4.4 Aggregated Data. Customer grants Vendor a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use de-identified, aggregated data derived from Customer Data for product improvement, benchmarking, and marketing purposes. Vendor will maintain technical measures to prevent re-identification.

4.5 Feedback. If Customer provides feedback regarding the Services, Vendor may use such feedback without restriction or obligation to Customer.

---

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Obligations. Each party will hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care as for its own confidential information (no less than reasonable care), and will use it only to perform obligations or exercise rights under this Agreement.

5.2 Exceptions. Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known without restriction; (c) is independently developed; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, with prompt prior notice where permitted.

5.3 Survival. Confidentiality obligations survive expiration or termination of this Agreement for two (2) years.

---

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 Vendor Warranties. Vendor warrants that: (a) the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the Subscription Term; and (b) Vendor will comply with all applicable laws in providing the Services.

6.2 Mutual Warranties. Each party warrants that it has full legal authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.3 Warranty Remedy. If the Services fail to conform to the warranties in Section 6.1(a), Customer will notify Vendor and Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity within 60 days. If uncorrected, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid, unused Fees.

6.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 6, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

---

7. DATA PROCESSING AND PRIVACY

7.1 Data Processing Agreement. The terms governing Vendor's processing of Personal Data on Customer's behalf are set forth in the Data Processing Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A, incorporated by reference. Any amendment to Exhibit A requires the written consent of both parties.

7.2 Compliance. Each party will comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws in connection with its activities under this Agreement.

---

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Consequential Damages. NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS OR DATA.

8.2 Aggregate Cap. EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

8.3 No Carve-Outs. The limitations in this Section 8 apply to all claims, including confidentiality breaches, data protection violations, and indemnification obligations.

---

9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Vendor Indemnification. Vendor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe any patent, copyright, or trademark.

9.2 Customer Indemnification. Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor from any third-party claim arising out of Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law.

9.3 Procedure. The indemnified party must promptly notify the indemnifying party, grant sole control of defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation.

---

10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

10.2 Auto-Renewal. Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least forty-five (45) days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term, each Order Form will automatically renew for successive one-year terms at then-current rates, subject to Section 3.3.

10.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice of a material breach if not cured within the notice period.

10.4 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for any reason on 60 days' written notice. Early termination does not entitle Customer to a refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

10.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access ceases. Vendor will provide a machine-readable export of Customer Data within 45 days and will delete Customer Data within 90 days.

10.6 Survival. Sections 1, 4.1, 4.2, 5, 6.4, 7, 8, 9, and 11 survive termination.

---

11. GENERAL

11.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in San Antonio, Texas.

11.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, all Order Forms, and Exhibits constitute the entire agreement and supersede all prior understandings.

11.3 Amendment. No amendment is valid unless signed in writing by both parties.

11.4 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

11.5 Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. The affected party must give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

11.6 Notices. Notices must be in writing, delivered by email with confirmation, overnight courier, or certified mail to addresses in the Order Form.
