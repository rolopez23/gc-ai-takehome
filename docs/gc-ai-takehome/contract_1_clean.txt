MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

Vendor: Orion Software Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 400 Technology Drive, Suite 200, Austin, TX 78701 ("Vendor"), and

Customer: Meridian Analytics Corp., a Delaware corporation, 1200 Market Street, Floor 8, Philadelphia, PA 19103 ("Customer").

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Authorized Users" means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents permitted to access the Services under this Agreement.
1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, content, and information submitted to or collected by the Services on Customer's behalf, including Personal Data.
1.3 "Documentation" means technical specifications, user guides, and written materials provided by Vendor describing the Services.
1.4 "Order Form" means a written ordering document executed by both parties specifying the Services, Fees, and Subscription Term.
1.5 "Output" means any content, report, analysis, or result generated by the Services using Customer Data as input.
1.6 "Personal Data" has the meaning given under applicable data protection laws.
1.7 "Services" means the software-as-a-service products and related services described in an applicable Order Form.
1.8 "Subscription Term" means the period during which Customer has purchased access to the Services, as specified in the Order Form.

---

2. SERVICES

2.1 Provision. Vendor will make the Services available to Customer and its Authorized Users during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement, the applicable Order Form, and the Documentation.

2.2 Uptime SLA. Vendor guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime availability, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 72 hours in advance. If Vendor fails to meet this target in any calendar month, Customer will receive a service credit equal to 10% of that month's pro-rated Fees for each full percentage point below 99.9%, up to a maximum credit of 30% of monthly Fees. Service credits are Customer's exclusive remedy for uptime failures and will be applied to the next invoice.

2.3 Support SLA. Vendor will provide technical support with the following response time commitments: (a) Critical (service unavailable or data at risk): initial response within 4 hours, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; (b) High (material feature impairment): response within 1 business day; (c) Standard: response within 3 business days. Business hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

2.4 Data Security Certifications. Vendor will maintain SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications throughout the Subscription Term and will provide current reports or executive summaries to Customer upon written request. Vendor will notify Customer within 10 business days if any certification lapses or is placed under review.

2.5 Subprocessors. Vendor maintains a current list of subprocessors used to provide the Services, which will be disclosed to Customer at execution and kept current. Vendor requires Customer's prior written consent before engaging any new subprocessor with access to Customer Data. Vendor will provide at least 30 days' advance written notice of any proposed new subprocessor. Customer may object in writing within 15 days of such notice, and the parties will work in good faith to resolve the objection.

2.6 Change Management. Vendor will provide at least 30 days' prior written notice of any material change to the features or functionality of the Services that would adversely affect Customer's use.

---

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Fees. Customer will pay the fees specified in the applicable Order Form ("Fees"). All Fees are in U.S. dollars and are non-refundable except as expressly provided in this Agreement.

3.2 Payment Terms. Vendor will invoice Customer per the billing schedule in the Order Form. Customer will pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of receipt (Net 45). Customer may dispute any invoice in good faith within 15 days of receipt without forfeiting obligations on undisputed amounts. Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month.

3.3 Price Increase Cap. Vendor may increase Fees at the start of any renewal Subscription Term upon at least 60 days' prior written notice. Annual Fee increases are capped at the lesser of 3% or the change in the U.S. Consumer Price Index for the preceding 12-month period. No Fee increases may be applied during a then-current Subscription Term without Customer's prior written consent.

3.4 Taxes. Customer is responsible for all applicable sales, use, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on Vendor's net income.

---

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

4.1 Vendor IP. Vendor retains all rights, title, and interest in and to the Services, Documentation, underlying technology, and improvements thereto.

4.2 Customer Data and Output. Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Data and all Output. Vendor acquires no ownership interest in Customer Data or Output under this Agreement.

4.3 License to Provide Services. Customer grants Vendor a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and process Customer Data solely to provide the Services during the Subscription Term.

4.4 Restrictions on Customer Data. Vendor will not: (a) use Customer Data to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI or machine learning model; (b) use Customer Data for competitive benchmarking; (c) sell, rent, or otherwise commercialize Customer Data; or (d) disclose Customer Data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Services or as required by applicable law.

4.5 Feedback. If Customer provides feedback regarding the Services, Vendor may use such feedback without restriction or obligation, provided it does not incorporate Customer's Confidential Information or identify Customer without consent.

---

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Obligations. Each party will hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care as for its own confidential information (no less than reasonable care), and will use it only to perform obligations or exercise rights under this Agreement. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information designated as confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential given the nature and circumstances of disclosure.

5.2 Exceptions. Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) was already known without restriction; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, with prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permissible.

5.3 Survival. Confidentiality obligations survive expiration or termination of this Agreement for three (3) years.

---

6. WARRANTIES

6.1 Vendor Warranties. Vendor warrants that: (a) the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation during the Subscription Term; (b) Vendor will provide the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards; (c) Vendor will comply with all applicable laws and regulations in connection with providing the Services; and (d) to Vendor's knowledge, the Services do not, as provided, infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

6.2 Mutual Warranties. Each party warrants that it has full legal authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.3 Warranty Remedy. If the Services fail to conform to the warranties in Section 6.1(a), Customer will notify Vendor in writing and Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity at no charge within 30 days. If Vendor cannot correct the non-conformity, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid, unused Fees.

6.4 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION 6, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

7.1 Data Processing Agreement. The terms governing Vendor's processing of Personal Data on Customer's behalf — including security measures, subprocessor obligations, data subject rights, and cross-border transfer mechanisms — are set forth in the Data Processing Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A, incorporated by reference. Any amendment to Exhibit A requires the written consent of both parties.

7.2 Compliance. Each party will comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws in connection with its activities under this Agreement.

8. AUDIT RIGHTS

8.1 Customer may, no more than once per calendar year and upon at least 30 days' prior written notice, audit or commission an independent third-party audit of Vendor's systems and records to verify compliance with this Agreement, including security and data processing obligations, at Customer's cost. Vendor will cooperate reasonably and provide access to relevant documentation and personnel. Customer will treat all audit findings as Vendor's Confidential Information.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 Vendor will maintain throughout the Subscription Term: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence; (b) professional liability / errors and omissions insurance of at least $2,000,000; and (c) cyber liability / technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence. Upon written request, Vendor will provide certificates of insurance evidencing such coverage.

---

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Consequential Damages. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY, IP, OR DATA PROTECTION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY WILL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, EXEMPLARY, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS OR DATA, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY.

10.2 Aggregate Cap. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 10.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT WILL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

10.3 Excluded Claims. The cap in Section 10.2 does not apply to: (a) indemnification obligations; (b) confidentiality breaches; (c) data protection violations; or (d) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Vendor Indemnification. Vendor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer and its officers, directors, and employees from any third-party claim alleging that the Services, used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, or arising from Vendor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11.2 Customer Indemnification. Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor and its officers, directors, and employees from any third-party claim arising out of Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or applicable law.

11.3 Procedure. The indemnified party must: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying party; (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of defense and settlement; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation. The indemnifying party may not settle any claim that imposes obligations on the indemnified party without prior written consent.

---

12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

12.2 Auto-Renewal. Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current Subscription Term, each Order Form will automatically renew for successive one-year terms at the then-current rates, subject to Section 3.3.

12.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form on 30 days' written notice of a material breach if not cured within the notice period.

12.4 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for any reason on 30 days' written notice to Vendor.

12.5 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer's access ceases. Vendor will provide a complete, machine-readable export of Customer Data within 30 days and will securely delete Customer Data within 60 days of termination, unless retention is required by applicable law. Upon request, Vendor will provide written certification of deletion.

12.6 Exit Assistance. Upon Customer's written request delivered before or at the time of termination notice, Vendor will provide reasonable transition and migration assistance for up to 90 days following the effective date of termination, at no additional charge, to facilitate orderly migration of Customer Data and continuity of operations.

12.7 Survival. Sections 1, 4.1, 4.2, 5, 6.4, 7, 10, 11, and 13 survive termination.

13. GENERAL

13.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles. Customer's preferred jurisdiction applies and any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

13.2 Dispute Resolution. Before initiating formal proceedings, the parties will: (a) attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute through senior-level negotiations for at least 30 days; then (b) submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by JAMS for up to 30 days; then (c) if unresolved, resolve the dispute through binding arbitration administered by JAMS under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, with a single arbitrator, conducted in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Either party may seek emergency injunctive or equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction without first completing the foregoing steps.

13.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, all Order Forms, and all Exhibits constitute the entire agreement between the parties and supersede all prior negotiations, representations, and understandings.

13.4 Amendment. No amendment is valid unless made in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

13.5 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, provided the assignee agrees in writing to be bound by the Agreement.

13.6 Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delays or failures in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided that financial hardship and reasonably foreseeable events are expressly excluded. The affected party must give prompt written notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

13.7 Notices. All notices must be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt, overnight courier, or certified mail to the addresses specified in the applicable Order Form.
